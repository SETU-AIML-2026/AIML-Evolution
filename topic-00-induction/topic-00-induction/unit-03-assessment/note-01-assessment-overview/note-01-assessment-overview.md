# Assessment Overview

The module is **100% continuous assessment**, split evenly between two pieces staged half a semester
apart. There is no terminal examination.

| | Assignment 1 | Assignment 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | Mid-module quiz | Final project — brief + artefact |
| **Weight** | 50% | 50% |
| **When** | Week 6 (sat in or immediately after the lab) | Week 12 showcase; submitted in the closing reading week |
| **Outcomes** | LO1, LO2, LO4, LO6 | LO3, LO5, LO7 |
| **Covers** | Weeks 1–5 | Weeks 7–11, built from Week 10 |
| **Format** | Timed online quiz | Live presentation + peer review + submitted brief and artefact |

## The shape of it

The quiz consolidates the first half while it is still fresh: the cycles and breakthroughs of AI,
classical ML and neural-network training, model families and frameworks, the science-grounded
architectures of Week 5, and the responsible-AI principles from Week 2.

The final project runs on the second half. It is announced in the Sprint 3 reading week as a
proposal, built across Weeks 10 and 11, and showcased in Week 12.

## Late submission, extenuating circumstances, academic integrity

Programme-level regulations apply. Use of AI tools in the final project is expected and encouraged —
this is a module about these tools — but must be **declared and described** in the critical brief.
See the Assignment 2 brief for what that declaration should contain.
