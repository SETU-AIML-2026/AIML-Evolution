# Assignment 1 — Mid-Module Quiz (50%)

**Week 6 · timed online · outcomes LO1, LO2, LO4, LO6**

## What it covers

Everything from Weeks 1 to 5:

- the cycles and breakthroughs of AI, and what the current wave got right (Week 1)
- responsible AI: where bias enters, fairness metrics, explainability, the EU AI Act as an
  engineering requirement (Week 2)
- classical ML and neural-network training: backpropagation, gradient descent, why depth became
  possible (Week 3)
- model families as inductive bias: CNNs, RNNs and LSTMs, transformers and attention (Week 4)
- science-grounded architectures: PINNs, neural operators, neural ODEs, equivariance, diffusion as
  thermodynamics (Week 5)

## Format

A mix of multiple-choice and auto-graded items, short structured responses, and **a brief applied
component** — interpreting a model output or a fairness metric rather than recalling a definition.

> **To confirm.** Number of items, time limit and the mark split across the three formats.

## Preparing

Revision opens in the Sprint 1 reading week and the Week 6 lab is a structured pre-quiz clinic:
worked examples, concept-check exercises and a live Q&A drawing the first five weeks together. The
quiz is sat within, or immediately after, that session.

The most efficient preparation is to re-run the five interactives rather than to re-read the slides.
Each one was chosen because it makes a quiz-relevant idea visible: the timeline for cycles, the Bias
& Fairness Explorer for thresholds and trade-offs, the Gradient Descent Explorer for learning rates
and convergence, the Convolution and Attention Visualisers for what each model family sees, and the
PINN Sandbox for physics-versus-data efficiency.
