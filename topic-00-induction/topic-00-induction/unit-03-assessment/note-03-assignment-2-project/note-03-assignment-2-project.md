# Assignment 2 — Final Project (50%)

[[toc]]

**Week 12 showcase, submitted in the closing reading week · outcomes LO3, LO5, LO7**

A **critical brief** plus a **working applied artefact**. Three things are asked of it.

## 1. Evaluate a scale-free or lean approach against a transformer baseline

Pick one: a state-space model, a distilled or quantized model, or a reasoning model that spends
compute at inference time. Run it against a comparable transformer baseline and report what you
measured — accuracy, latency, memory, cost, or all four across a range of sequence lengths.

The claim being tested is the module's own thesis. Evidence beats assertion, and a negative result
that is honestly measured is worth more than a favourable one that is not.

## 2. Analyse its impact on a specific workflow

Take one concrete software-development or system-design workflow and analyse what your approach
changes about it. Specific beats general: *code review on a private repository that cannot leave the
building* is a workflow; *software engineering* is not.

## 3. Assess one emerging trend for a named use case

One trend from Week 11 — federated learning, edge AI, neuromorphic, quantum ML, agentic or world
models, or AI for scientific discovery — assessed for a named use case. Substance against hype is the
skill being examined.
