# Reading

## Core texts

- **Russell, S. & Norvig, P. (2020)** *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach.* The standard
  reference for the field's shape and history. Weeks 1 and 3.
- **Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y. & Courville, A. (2016)** *Deep Learning.* Training, optimisation and
  the model families. Weeks 3 and 4.
- **Bronstein, M. et al.** *Geometric Deep Learning.* Symmetry and equivariance as inductive bias —
  the theoretical backing for Week 5.

## Current literature

The frontier strands are too recent for textbooks. Reading for Weeks 5, 8, 9 and 11 is drawn from
current literature on **physics-informed machine learning**, **state-space models** and **model
compression**, and is refreshed each delivery to track the field.

> Per-week readings are posted in the relevant topic. Expect two or three papers a week in the
> frontier weeks, and expect at least one of them to have been published since the last delivery of
> this module.

## How to read the papers

You are not being asked to reproduce results. For each paper, be able to answer three questions:
what assumption about the data does this architecture encode, what does that assumption buy in data
efficiency or compute, and where does it break. That is also, not coincidentally, the shape of the
final-project brief.
