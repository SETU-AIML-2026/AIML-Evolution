# The Interactive Materials

Eight browser-based interactives run through the module, embedded directly in Tutors. They are
client-side, so they work in the same browser tab as the notes, with nothing to install.

They are not decoration. Each one exists because a specific idea in the module is easier to *see*
than to read, and several are the fastest route to revision.

| # | Interactive | Week | Strand | Serves | Makes visible |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **AI Evolution Timeline** | 1 | History | LO1 | The field's cycles, at macro scale |
| 2 | **Bias & Fairness Explorer** | 2 | Responsible AI | LO6 | Thresholds, metrics and the trade-offs between them |
| 3 | **Gradient Descent Explorer** | 3 | Foundations | LO2 | Learning rate, convergence, local minima |
| 4 | **Convolution + Attention Visualiser** | 4 | Architecture | LO2, LO3 | What each model family actually "sees" |
| 5 | **PINN Sandbox** | 5 | Science-inspired | LO2 | Physics in the loss, against a data-only baseline |
| 6 | **Attention-vs-SSM Scaling Visualiser** | 8 | Scale-free | LO3 | Quadratic versus linear cost as sequences grow |
| 7 | **Compression Trade-off Explorer** | 9 | Lean models | LO3, LO7 | Size against accuracy under quantization and distillation |
| 8 | **Protein / Materials Explorer** | 11 | Science-inspired | LO7 | How to read an AI-predicted scientific structure |
