# The Module at a Glance

Quick Review

## Where this module sits

| | |
|---|---|
| **Module** | The Evolution of Machine Learning and AI |
| **Credits** | 5 ECTS, Semester 1 |
| **Award** | Award Certificate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineering (30 ECTS) |
| **Parent programme** | Higher Diploma in Science in Computer Science (NFQ Level 8) |
| **Delivery** | Fully online — live lectures, asynchronous content, self-paced labs |
| **Effort** | 24 lecture hours + 24 practical hours + 87 hours independent learning |

## What you do each week

Every teaching week carries a **2h live online lecture** and a **2h practical lab**, with self-paced
asynchronous content here on Tutors. Twelve teaching weeks are grouped into four three-week sprints,
each closed by a reading week. Two of those sprints close on an assessment rather than new content.

The 87 hours of independent learning is where the module actually happens: reading, running the labs
properly rather than skimming them, and — from Week 10 — building the final-project artefact.

## Learning outcomes

Short forms of the descriptor outcomes. The full wording is in the module descriptor; the mapping of
outcomes to weeks and assessments is in **Constructive Alignment** in the Semester Map.

| LO | Outcome | Assessed in |
|---|---|---|
| LO1 | Trace the historical development of AI and ML | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO2 | Characterise the training of model families, including diffusion and science-grounded models | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO3 | Critically evaluate modern LLM architecture and function, including lean and scale-free models | Final project (Wk 12) |
| LO4 | Identify tools and frameworks in contemporary AI/ML | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO5 | Analyse the influence of LLMs on software development workflows | Final project (Wk 12) |
| LO6 | Summarise ethical implications: bias, fairness, explainability | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO7 | Evaluate emerging trends: science-inspired, lean, edge, federated, quantum | Final project (Wk 12) |

## Assessment in one line

100% continuous, split evenly: a **mid-module quiz in Week 6** (50%) covering the foundations and the
science strand, and a **final project showcased in Week 12** (50%) on the lean and LLM frontier.
Details in the Assessment unit.
