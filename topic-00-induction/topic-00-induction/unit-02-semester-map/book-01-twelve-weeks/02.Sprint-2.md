# Sprint 2 — Architecture as Inductive Bias → the Science Frontier

**Weeks 4–6.** One idea carries this sprint: an architecture is an assumption about the world. Week 4
establishes it for the standard model families; Week 5 takes it to its strongest form by building
physical law into the model itself. Week 6 consolidates everything so far.

## Week 4 — Model families: structure is an assumption about the world

`Indicative 4, 5` · `LO2, LO3` · `topic-04-model-families`

**Lecture (2h).** How connectivity yields capability — CNNs (spatial locality), RNNs and LSTMs
(sequence), transformers and attention (relations). The key idea carried into Week 5: an architecture
encodes assumptions about its data, and when those assumptions match reality, models need less data
and generalise better.

**Lab (2h).** Visualise what a CNN learns with the Convolution / Feature-Map Visualiser; explore
token relations with the Attention Visualiser.

## Week 5 — Science-inspired AI: building physical law into models

`Frontier — extends 4, 5` · `LO2` · `topic-05-science-inspired-ai`

**Lecture (2h).** Inductive bias drawn from science. Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) that
satisfy governing equations; neural operators (FNO, DeepONet) that learn PDE solution maps; neural
ODEs for continuous dynamics; equivariant and geometric deep learning (symmetry); diffusion models
understood as non-equilibrium thermodynamics; thermodynamics-informed GNNs. Why physical priors cut
data needs and improve reliability.

**Lab (2h).** Build a small PINN that solves an ODE or PDE — a damped oscillator, or the heat
equation — in the PINN Sandbox, and compare it against a data-only baseline for accuracy and data
efficiency.

## Week 6 — Quiz: foundations and the science frontier

`Assessment week` · `LO1, LO2, LO4, LO6` · `topic-06-quiz-and-review`

**Quiz (online, timed).** The first assessment piece, 50% of the module. Covers the history and
cycles of AI, classical ML and neural-network training, model families and frameworks, the
science-grounded architectures of Week 5, and the responsible-AI principles from Week 2. Full brief
in the Assessment unit.

**Lab (2h).** Structured pre-quiz review clinic: worked examples, concept-check exercises and a live
Q&A drawing the first five weeks together. The quiz is sat within, or immediately after, this session.
