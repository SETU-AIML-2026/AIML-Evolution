# Sprint 4 — Systems, Frontier Trends, Final Project

**Weeks 10–12.** From models to systems, then out to the frontier, then your own artefact.

## Week 10 — LLMs in software engineering and system design

`Indicative 8` · `LO5` · `topic-10-llms-in-software-engineering`

**Lecture (2h).** Agentic systems, AI-assisted development, retrieval-augmented generation,
embeddings and vector databases — and how to architect systems around lean local models plus frontier
APIs. The augmentation-not-replacement thesis, carried over from the Software Engineering
certificate.

**Lab (2h).** Build a RAG application powered by a local lean model; complete a real task with an AI
coding agent. Final-project build begins here.

## Week 11 — Frontier and emerging trends: from AI-for-science to the edge

`Indicative 10 + frontier` · `LO5, LO7` · `topic-11-frontier-trends`

**Lecture (2h).** AI as a scientific instrument leads the session — AlphaFold 2→3, GNoME materials
discovery, protein binder design, weather and climate models, science foundation models — with a
critical view that AI tends to complement rather than replace experimental work. Then the deployment
frontier: federated learning, edge AI, neuromorphic and spiking systems, quantum ML, agentic and
world models. How the science-inspired and lean currents are converging, and how to weigh substance
against hype.

**Lab (2h).** Explore an AI-predicted scientific structure with the Protein / Materials Explorer; run
a federated-learning or edge-ML demonstration. Continue the final-project build.

## Week 12 — Final project: build, showcase, reflect

`Assessment week` · `LO3, LO5, LO7` · `topic-12-final-project`

**Showcase (live).** The culminating assessment, 50% of the module. Present a working lean/LLM
artefact and a critical brief, followed by structured peer review. Full brief in the Assessment unit.

**Synthesis (closing).** A short wrap-up drawing the two frontier currents together — grounding
models in science, and shrinking them to run anywhere — and reflecting on the decade ahead.

> **Reading week.** Final-project submission.
