# Sprint 1 — Fast Foundations + Responsible AI Up Front

**Weeks 1–3.** History compressed to a single orientation week, ethics installed before anything is
built, and the whole of classical ML and neural network training delivered in one applied week.

## Week 1 — The shape of the field: cycles, breakthroughs, and what the current wave got right

`Indicative 1, 5` · `LO1` · `topic-01-shape-of-the-field`

**Lecture (2h).** A brisk tour of the evolution — pioneers, two winters, resurgence — framed as *why
the field moves in cycles* and what the modern wave actually got right: data, compute and
architecture arriving together. Ends by setting the module thesis: scaling worked, but the frontier is
now moving past brute force toward science-grounded and lean systems.

**Lab (2h).** The AI Evolution Timeline as a 30-minute macro orientation. Then environment setup:
Python, Colab, HuggingFace, Ollama, GitHub.

## Week 2 — Responsible AI, before we build anything

`Indicative 9` · `LO6` · `topic-02-responsible-ai`

**Lecture (2h).** Bias, fairness, accountability, transparency and explainability introduced as a
design constraint rather than an afterthought. Where bias enters: data, objective, deployment.
Documented real-world harms. The EU AI Act and responsible-AI principles read as engineering
requirements. This lens is applied in every subsequent week.

**Lab (2h).** Audit a dataset and a model for biased outcomes with the Bias & Fairness Explorer;
apply explainability tools (SHAP, LIME); watch how moving a decision threshold moves the fairness
metrics.

## Week 3 — How machines learn, fast: classical ML, neural nets, training

`Indicative 2, 3, 7` · `LO2, LO4` · `topic-03-how-machines-learn`

**Lecture (2h).** Compressed but applied: classical ML (regression, trees, SVMs) → the perceptron and
multilayer networks → backpropagation and gradient descent, intuitively → why GPUs and big data
unlocked depth. PyTorch introduced as the working framework.

**Lab (2h).** Train a classical model and a small neural net in PyTorch; use the Gradient Descent
Explorer to see learning-rate effects, convergence and local minima.

> **Reading week.** Consolidation. Quiz revision begins.
