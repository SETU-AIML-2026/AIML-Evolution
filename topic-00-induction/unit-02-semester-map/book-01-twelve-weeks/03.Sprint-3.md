# Sprint 3 — LLMs, the Limits of Scale, and the Lean Turn

**Weeks 7–9.** The scaling era, then the two escapes from it: change the architecture so cost stops
being quadratic, or shrink the model until it runs on your own hardware.

## Week 7 — Transformers and LLMs: the scaling era and where it strains

`Indicative 5, 6, 7` · `LO3, LO4` · `topic-07-transformers-and-llms`

**Lecture (2h).** Transformer recap → LLMs: pretraining, fine-tuning, RLHF, tokenisation, emergent
ability. Open versus proprietary models — Llama, ChatGPT, DeepSeek. Then scaling laws and their
limits: diminishing returns, cost, energy, data ceilings. This motivates the lean turn that follows.

**Lab (2h).** Run LLMs locally with Ollama; use the Tokeniser Explorer; compare open and proprietary
models on the same prompts.

## Week 8 — Scale-free LLMs: beyond brute-force attention

`Frontier — extends 6, 7` · `LO3, LO7` · `topic-08-scale-free-llms`

**Lecture (2h).** The efficiency frontier. The quadratic-attention bottleneck → state-space models
(S4, Mamba, Mamba-2), RetNet, RWKV, and linear and attention-free architectures with constant memory
and linear-time inference. Mixture-of-experts and sparsity for capacity without cost.
Test-time-compute and reasoning models that scale inference rather than parameters.

**Lab (2h).** Run and benchmark a Mamba/SSM model against a comparable transformer; measure latency
and memory across sequence lengths with the Attention-vs-SSM Scaling Visualiser.

## Week 9 — Lean LLMs: small, cheap, private, on-device

`Frontier — extends 6, 7 + indicative 10` · `LO3, LO4, LO7` · `topic-09-lean-llms`

**Lecture (2h).** Making models lean: knowledge distillation, including transformer→recurrent
distillation; low-bit quantization from 4-bit down to 1-bit; pruning; and parameter-efficient
fine-tuning with LoRA and QLoRA. Small language models; edge and TinyML deployment; energy use and
neuromorphic/spiking approaches. Why lean matters — cost, privacy, sustainability, access.

**Lab (2h).** Quantize and LoRA-fine-tune a small model; deploy it under realistic edge constraints;
use the Compression Trade-off Explorer to weigh size against accuracy.
