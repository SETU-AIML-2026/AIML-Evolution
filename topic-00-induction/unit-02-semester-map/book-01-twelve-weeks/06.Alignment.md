# Constructive Alignment — Outcomes to Weeks to Assessment

| Module LO | Where addressed | Assessed in |
|---|---|---|
| LO1 — Trace historical development of AI/ML | Week 1 | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO2 — Characterise training of model families, including diffusion and science-grounded | Weeks 3–5 | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO3 — Critically evaluate modern LLM architecture and function, including lean and scale-free | Weeks 4, 7–9 | Final project (Wk 12) |
| LO4 — Identify tools and frameworks in contemporary AI/ML | Weeks 3, 7, 9 | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO5 — Analyse LLM influence on software development workflows | Weeks 10, 12 | Final project (Wk 12) |
| LO6 — Summarise ethical implications: bias, fairness, explainability | Week 2, threaded | Quiz (Wk 6) |
| LO7 — Evaluate emerging trends: science-inspired, lean, edge, federated, quantum | Weeks 8–9, 11 | Final project (Wk 12) |

Two consequences of the ordering are worth stating explicitly:

- Moving bias and fairness to **Week 2** means LO6 is taught, practised in a lab, and applied as a
  lens in Weeks 3–5 before the Week 6 quiz assesses it.
- The quiz assesses the four outcomes that are complete by Week 5 (LO1, LO2, LO4, LO6). The final
  project assesses the three that depend on the second half (LO3, LO5, LO7). No outcome is assessed
  before it has been taught.
