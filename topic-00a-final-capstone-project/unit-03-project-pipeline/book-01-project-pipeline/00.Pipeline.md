# The Project Pipeline

Your project is built one small step a week, on your own, following the **Lecture and Lab Plan, Weeks 7 to 11** (the PDF in this unit) and your personal roadmap on Moodle. Each recorded lecture teaches the week's AI topic, explains how it connects to your project, and shows the step on the class example in 20 minutes. At home, Part A of the lab (15 minutes) is a small task on the topic, and Part B (45 minutes) is the project step on your own law.

## Four rules

1. **Your own law, your own work.** The one from your idea form, with your feedback applied. The project is individual: no pairs or groups. Sharing a law with classmates is fine; the work is yours alone.
2. **No collected data.** The model learns from the law. Any "examples" are made from your answer sheet.
3. **AI as a helper.** Keep an AI-use log from Week 7 onwards; you will need it for section 8 and Appendix B of your report, and for the interview.
4. **Stuck for 40 minutes?** Stop, and post in the channel.
