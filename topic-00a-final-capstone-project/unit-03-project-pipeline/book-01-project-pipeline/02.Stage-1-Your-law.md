# Stage 1 · Your Law and Its Answer Sheet (Week 7, from 2 November)

**Goal:** your law written so a computer can use it, and the right answer to test your model against from now on.

**In the lecture:** large language models, then the 20-minute walkthrough in Google Colab on the sourdough starter. Part A of the Week 7 lab (15 minutes) covers the topic; this page is Part B.

## Do

1. Open `capstone_step1.ipynb` in Google Colab (Week 7 lab; every click is in the Week 7 core booklet, Chapters 4 to 6).
2. Run Step 0 and Part 1, the class example.
3. Go to the part for your law's shape: one thing changing (Part 2), two or three things changing each other (Part 3), or changing along a line (Part 4).
4. Replace every line marked `<--`: what changes, the unit of time, your numbers with their units and sources, your starting value, how long to look, and your law as one line.
5. Run it and download `my_answer_sheet.png`.
6. Start your AI-use log: a simple list of what you asked an AI tool, what you used, and what you changed.

## You have it when

- [ ] The answer sheet for your own law runs and looks sensible.
- [ ] If you have both an exact formula and the solver, the two lines lie on top of each other.
- [ ] Every number has a unit and a source.

**Hand in:** the Week 7 form (Week 7 lab on Tutors).

**Common problems:** a sign the wrong way round (the answer grows when it should shrink); numbers far from 1 (change the units); the formula and the solver disagree (check the law line first).
