# Help and Common Questions

**Do I need to collect any data?** No. Never. Any examples are made from your own answer sheet.

**Do I need to write code from scratch?** No. Each week's notebook has the class example and clearly marked lines for your law.

**Can I work with a classmate?** No. The project is individual from start to finish.

**My idea is the same as a classmate's. Is that allowed?** Yes. Your person, your numbers, your question and your work make it yours. Do not share code or text.

**Can I change my idea?** Before the Week 8 lecture (9 November), yes: send the idea form again. After that, only with the lecturer's agreement.

**What if my model never passes my success test?** Report it honestly, with what you tried. A well-explained failure, with fair checks, can still earn high marks.

**Can I use ChatGPT, Copilot or Claude?** Yes, as a helper. Log what you used it for, check everything, and be ready to explain every line.

**Word or LaTeX?** Either. Use whichever you are comfortable with; both templates have the same sections. Submit a PDF.

**Is there a showcase or presentation?** No. The report (90%) and the in-person interview (10%) are the whole project mark.

**I am stuck.** Stop after 40 minutes and post in the channel, with a screenshot of the problem.
