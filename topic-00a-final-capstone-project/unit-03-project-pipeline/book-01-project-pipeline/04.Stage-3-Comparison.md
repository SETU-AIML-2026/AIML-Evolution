# Stage 3 · The Comparison (Week 9, from 16 November)

**Goal:** the comparison the project is marked on: your model against an ordinary model that learns from examples.

**In the lecture:** smaller language models, then the walkthrough on the sourdough starter. Part A of the Week 9 lab (15 minutes) covers the topic; this page is Part B.

## Do

1. Open `capstone_step3.ipynb` (Week 9 lab) and put in your law, numbers and start.
2. Train the example-learner on 4, 8, 16, 32 and 64 points taken from your answer sheet, with a little noise. Same size and training time as your model: only what it learns from changes.
3. Fill the table: accuracy (error against the answer sheet), speed per answer, size.
4. Run the outside-the-range test: train on the first part of the time, score on the rest.
5. Shrink your model (fewer numbers, fewer digits) until it fails your success test, and keep the last version that passed.
6. Answer the comparison question you chose on the idea form (your roadmap repeats it) in one sentence with a number.
