# Stage 2 · Your First Model (Week 8, from 9 November)

**Goal:** a model that has learned your law with no data, judged by a success test you set in advance.

**In the lecture:** faster language models, then the walkthrough on the sourdough starter. Part A of the Week 8 lab (15 minutes) covers the topic; this page is Part B.

## Do

1. Open `capstone_step2.ipynb` (Week 8 lab) and put in your law, numbers and start.
2. Write your success test as a number before training, for example "the time to reach 60 °C is within one minute of the answer sheet".
3. Train, and watch the loss curve fall and flatten.
4. Plot the model on top of your answer sheet and read the error.
5. If it fails, try the fixes in order, one at a time: numbers near 1; more checking points; a shorter time range; smaller steps.
6. Write two or three sentences on where your model could go wrong. They become part of section 6 of your report.

## You have it when

- [ ] The model trains and its picture is saved.
- [ ] You have the error, the training time and the model size.
- [ ] You know whether it passes your success test.
