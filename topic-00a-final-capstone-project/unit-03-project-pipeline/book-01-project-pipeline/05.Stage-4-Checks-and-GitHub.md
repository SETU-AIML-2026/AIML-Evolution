# Stage 4 · Three Checks and Your Repository (Week 10, from 23 November)

**Goal:** know where your model can be trusted, and put everything where someone else can run it.

**In the lecture:** AI for building software, then the walkthrough on the sourdough starter. Part A of the Week 10 lab (15 minutes) covers the topic; this page is Part B.

## Do

1. Open `capstone_step4.ipynb` (Week 10 lab) and put in your law, numbers and start.
2. **New points:** score your model at times it was never checked at.
3. **Something that must always be true:** ask what can never happen, what must stay the same, and what can only go one way for your law. Turn one answer into a test.
4. **The wrong-law test:** change one number in your law by 20%. How far does the answer move? Would your model's own score have warned you?
5. Write one sentence on what your law leaves out: your model cannot know it, so you must say it.
6. Optional: find one unknown number in your law from a few noisy readings, and compare it with the true value.
7. Make your repository from the **repository template** (Templates, in this topic), add your notebooks and results, and fill in the README and the AI-use log.
8. Test it yourself: open your repository in a fresh Colab session and run it from the README.

## You have it when

- [ ] All three checks have a result and a sentence explaining it.
- [ ] Your repository runs from its README in a fresh Colab session.

**Hand in:** the Week 10 form (Week 10 lab): your repository link, each check's result, your sentence on what the law leaves out, and how you used AI.
