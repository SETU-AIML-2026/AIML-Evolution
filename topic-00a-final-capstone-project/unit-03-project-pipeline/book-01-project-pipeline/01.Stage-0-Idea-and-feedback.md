# Stage 0 · Your Idea and Its Feedback

**When:** after Week 6.

You have sent the capstone idea form, and everyone receives written feedback on Moodle with one of three outcomes: **go ahead**, **go ahead with changes**, or **new idea needed**. The page *Feedback on Your Ideas* in this topic explains each one and lists what we learned from all the ideas.

## Do

1. Read your feedback on Moodle: your idea feedback, then your personal roadmap, the steps for your own project from Week 7 to Week 12.
2. If it says *go ahead with changes*, note the changes: you make them in Stage 1, on your problem card.
3. If it says *new idea needed*, choose a new idea from the student guide and send the idea form again **before the Week 8 lecture (9 November)**.
4. If anything in your feedback is unclear, post in the channel.

## You are ready when

- [ ] You know your law, in words.
- [ ] You know the person who would use your answer.
- [ ] You know whether you check against an exact formula or the solver.
