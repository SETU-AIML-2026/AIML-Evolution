# How It Is Marked

Your project is marked out of 100 and counts **50%** of the module. All marking is done by Dr Indrakshi Dey. The report carries 90 marks and the interview 10.

| Part | Weight | Learning outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| R1 · The law, the answer sheet and the model | 15% | LO3, LO7 |
| R2 · The benchmarked comparison | 20% | LO3, LO7 |
| R3 · Checks and limits | 15% | LO3, LO7 |
| R4 · Critical discussion: the person, LLMs, your way of working, the trend | 25% | LO3, LO5, LO7 |
| R5 · Reproducibility and process: repository, README, AI-use log, weekly steps | 10% | LO5 |
| R6 · Clarity and presentation of the report | 5% | LO3, LO5, LO7 |
| IV · Personal interview (in person) | 10% | LO3, LO5, LO7 |

**What top marks (70% and above) look like**

- **R1:** the law, numbers, units and sources are correct and explained; the answer sheet is made two ways where possible; the model is reported with its loss curve, error and success test.
- **R2:** a complete, fair comparison, with a clear finding backed by a number.
- **R3:** all three checks run and explained in terms of what they mean for your person.
- **R4:** real insight in all four parts: the person, LLMs, your way of working with AI tools, and the trend.
- **R5:** the repository runs from its README with a fixed seed; regular commits; a detailed AI-use log; every weekly step handed in on time.
- **R6:** clear, concise, every figure and table labelled and referred to, sources cited.
- **Interview:** you explain every part of your own code and results clearly, and your AI-use log matches what you describe.

Each part is marked against the module's bands: 70–100% excellent critical insight; 60–69% good analysis with practical application; 50–59% sound understanding; 40–49% basic understanding; below 40% incomplete or inaccurate. The full rubric is in the PDF in this unit.
