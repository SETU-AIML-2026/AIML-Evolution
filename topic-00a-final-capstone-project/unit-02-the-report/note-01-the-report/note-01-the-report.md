# The Project Report

**Assignment 2 · 50% of the module · Learning outcomes 3, 5 and 7 · individual work**

## What you submit

Your final project is assessed in two parts:

| Part | Weight | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **The report** | 90% | One detailed report, as one PDF, written in Word or LaTeX. It holds everything: your law, your answer sheet, your model, your comparison, your checks, your discussion, your repository link and your AI-use log. |
| **The interview** | 10% | Ten minutes, in person, one to one, about your own work, in the week of Monday 11 January 2027. |

There is no showcase and no separate slides, brief or cover sheet.

## What the report contains

Follow the **Sample Project Report Outline** in this unit. In short:

1. The person and the problem
2. The law
3. The answer sheet
4. The model
5. The comparison
6. Checks and limits
7. Large language models and this problem
8. How I worked: AI tools in my workflow
9. Where this is heading
10. Conclusion

Then references, Appendix A (your repository link and how to run it) and Appendix B (your AI-use log). Aim for about 3,500 words (3,000 to 4,000), not counting tables, figure captions, references and appendices.

## How you build it

Weeks 7 to 10 produce the figures, tables and numbers; Week 11 (from 30 November) is the full draft; Week 12 (from 7 December) and the Christmas break are for finishing it. The report is due by 23:59 on Wednesday 6 January 2027, and the interviews are in the week of Monday 11 January 2027. Your personal roadmap on Moodle lists the steps for your own law.

## Word or LaTeX

Use the **Word template** or the **LaTeX template** (Templates and hand-in, in this topic). The LaTeX template also works in Overleaf. Either way, submit one PDF named **StudentNumber_Surname_Report.pdf** on the **Final submission** card, by **23:59 on Wednesday 6 January 2027**.

## Using AI tools

You may use AI tools as a helper, not a replacement. Keep an AI-use log in your repository, copy it into Appendix B, explain in section 8 how AI shaped your work, and be ready to explain every line in the interview.
