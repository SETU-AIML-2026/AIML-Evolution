Five steps, about twenty minutes each. Every step tells you **what to run**,
**what you should see**, and **what to write down**. That is the whole lab.

The steps follow the one picture from the lecture, the pipeline:

> **World → Data → Labels → Objective → Model → Threshold → Deploy**

Each step is one stop on that pipeline, and each page of this book is one step.

## One rule about the data

The dataset is a made-up loan file, `data/lending_data.csv`: 8,000 people, two groups **A** and **B**.

> **Both groups are equally good at repaying loans.** That was written into the data on purpose.

So every difference you measure between A and B was **manufactured**, by one of the four
mechanisms the lecture named: under-sampling, historical disadvantage, a proxy column,
and biased labels. If you ever catch yourself thinking "B is just riskier": no. You
have the proof that they are not, and you will see it in Step 1.
