# The Deliverables

Two documents, copied from templates in the lab folder:

```bash
cp model_card_template.md model_card.md       # graded
cp datasheet_template.md  datasheet.md        # optional this week
```

`make_submission.py` looks for `model_card.md` by name and will stop if it is
missing. That is deliberate.

## Why these are not paperwork

Both are the mechanism by which a decision acquires a name. An undocumented
system cannot be audited, cannot be safely inherited, and cannot be defended
when challenged eighteen months later by someone who was not in the room.

A model card takes an afternoon. It is the artefact a regulator, a buyer, an
auditor and your successor all ask for first.

## What carries the marks

Three sections, and none of them is the accuracy number:

**The fairness criterion you committed to.** Name one. Demographic parity,
equalised odds, equal opportunity, calibration or predictive parity. Not "we
considered fairness".

**The reasoning.** Start from the harm, not the metric. Write one sentence
describing what going wrong looks like for a person refused this loan. The
sentence usually points at a family. Then say why that family and not the others.

**What you gave up.** In numbers from your own run. Not "there is a trade-off"
but "committing to equalised odds left a 0.1296 predictive-parity gap and cost
0.0093 of aggregate accuracy against the baseline."

That third section is the one most students skip and the one that separates a
professional document from a compliance gesture.

## Disaggregated performance

The model card carries performance **by group**, not in aggregate. Your table
from page 3 goes here, counts included, so a reader can see where the estimates
are thin.

This is the cheapest responsible-AI practice in the module: no new algorithm, no
new data, about twenty lines of code. It is also the one most often skipped,
because an aggregate number is flattering and a table of subgroup numbers rarely
is.

## The datasheet

Optional this week, and the other half of the Week 12 format. It records why the
data was created, who funded it, what the instances represent, how it was
collected and from whom, with what consent, how it was cleaned, and — the part
people forget — **what tasks it must not be used for**.

For this dataset the honest datasheet is unusual and instructive: you know
exactly what was done to it, because the generator is in the folder. Write the
four mechanisms into the limitations section and say what each one does to any
conclusion drawn from the file.
