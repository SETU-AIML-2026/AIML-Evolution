# The Dataset, and What Was Done To It

```bash
python run_lab.py        # the whole audit; figures into ./figures
```

The dataset is a synthetic lending file: 8,000 applicants, seven permitted
features, one protected attribute `group`, and an outcome column `repaid`.

## The one rule

**Both groups have identical latent creditworthiness.** There is no difference
in ability to repay. It is written into the generator:

```
mean latent, group A: -0.017
mean latent, group B: -0.005
```

Every disparity you are about to measure was manufactured by one of four
mechanisms, each corresponding to a stage of the booklet's pipeline:

| mechanism | what it does | stage | booklet |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sampling_bias` | Group B is 20% of rows rather than 50%, so it exerts little pressure on the loss | 2. Data | Ch. 4, representation bias |
| `historical_bias` | At identical creditworthiness, group B has lower recorded income and savings and shorter credit histories | 1. World | Ch. 4, historical bias |
| `proxy` | `postcode_score` is strongly associated with group membership | 2. Data | Ch. 5, proxy variables |
| `label_bias` | Some group B borrowers who did repay are recorded as having defaulted, because arrears were recorded more aggressively | 3. Labels | Ch. 5, label bias |

Each is a keyword argument to `make_lending_data()`. Turn them on and off
independently. That is Exercise 1.

## The population table

The first table of any audit, and the one Chapter 4 says to produce before
anything else:

| group | n | share | repaid rate | mean income | mean savings | mean credit history (months) | mean postcode score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 6372 | 0.796 | 0.547 | 31,840 | 4,197 | 83.5 | 0.717 |
| B | 1628 | 0.204 | 0.437 | 25,756 | 2,333 | 53.6 | 0.064 |

Read it before you read anything else. Group B is a fifth of the file, earns
less on paper, has saved less on paper, has a third less recorded credit
history, and repays eleven points less often.

None of that is ability. All of it is mechanism.

## The discipline this enforces

If you ever catch yourself explaining a gap by saying *"group B is just
riskier"*, open the generator. You wrote it. They aren't.

That sentence is the whole reason the dataset is synthetic rather than real.
On a real file you can always retreat into "the base rates genuinely differ".
Here you cannot, because you have the latent variable and it says they do not.

> The `repaid` column is not ability to repay. It is a record of what the
> institution wrote down. That is the Chapter 5 lesson, and by the end of this
> lab it will be the fix that works.
