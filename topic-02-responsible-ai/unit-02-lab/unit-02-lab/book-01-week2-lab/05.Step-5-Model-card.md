# Step 5 · The model card and the datasheet

*≈ 20 min · lecture: pipeline stage Deploy, "who acts on it, and can they refuse it?"*

*These two files are used in this step only. Steps 1 to 4 never need them.*

## What you are doing

Before a model is used on real people, someone writes a **model card** (what the model
is for, who it may harm, which fairness trade-off was chosen and why) and a
**datasheet** for the data it was trained on. Both are written for you, using the numbers
from Steps 1–4:

- `model_card.md`: six boxes.
- `datasheet.md`: what is in `data/lending_data.csv` and how it was made.

## Do this

Open `model_card.md`. Read box 4 (the fairness criterion, and why that one for a *loan*)
and box 5 (the fix chosen and its cost in numbers). Compare box 5 with your Step 4 table.

Then open `datasheet.md` and compare its group counts and repayment rates with your
Step 1 table.

## What you should see

Every number in both files matches your run:

| where | number |
|---|---|
| model card, box 5 | accuracy 0.698 (baseline 0.699); approval gap 0.033 (baseline 0.203) |
| datasheet, composition | group B 20.4 %; repayment rate A 0.547, B 0.437 |

If one differs, change it to yours.
