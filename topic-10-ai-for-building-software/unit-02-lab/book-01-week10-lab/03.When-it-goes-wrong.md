# When It Goes Wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| `KeyError` with your number's name | `wrong_number` must be the exact name, in quotes: `"a"` |
| Test 2 broken at many moments | Check the direction of your rule (`>=` or `<=`); if right, the model really breaks it: say so |
| Test 2 broken only near zero | Allow a small margin, such as `y >= -1e-3`, and say so |
| The wrong-law test barely moves the answer | Your answer depends little on that number: a finding. Try another |
| GitHub upload fails | Upload a few files at a time; leave out files over 25 MB |
