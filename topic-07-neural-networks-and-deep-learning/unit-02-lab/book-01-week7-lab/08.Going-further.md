# Going Further (optional)

For people who finished the six exercises and want more. Each is one line in the notebook.

- **Fix a broken run.** `ls.broken_run()` trains on the credit data with three problems: the wrong sign in the update, unscaled inputs, and a step size of 5. Fix one at a time with `ls.broken_run(fix_sign=True)`, then add `fix_scaling=True`, then `fix_step=True`. Why does the accuracy sit at exactly 0.779 until the last fix?
- **A cure for memorising.** `ls.memorising()` trains a big network on 80 noisy dots; `ls.memorising(decay=0.01)` adds weight decay. Compare the gap between practice and new dots.
- **Catch more defaulters.** `ls.thresholds(my_backward)` shows what lowering the cut-off from 0.5 to 0.2 does to the share of defaulters caught and the share of flags that are right. Compare with your Week 2 results.
- **The five lines, for real.** With PyTorch installed, `ls.five_lines()` trains the same network in PyTorch; without it, it prints the five lines. Match each to the four loop steps.
