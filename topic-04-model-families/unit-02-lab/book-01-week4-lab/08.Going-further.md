# Going Further (optional)

For people who finished the six exercises and want more.

- **The browser toys.** `interactive/convolution-feature-map-visualiser.html`, `attention-visualiser.html`, `sequence-memory-explorer.html`. Double-click; no install.
- **Break it on purpose.** In `inductive_bias.py`, shuffle whole columns only and predict what the CNN does before running. In `sequences.py`, set the LSTM's `forget_bias` to 0.0 and explain the failure with the half-life table. In `attention.py`, remove the `sqrt(d)` and raise `d_model` to 128; name the failure.
- **The full run.** `python run_lab.py` reproduces every number in the lecture (about four minutes; `--quick` for one).
- **PyTorch.** `torch_models.py` builds the same three families in PyTorch. Optional.
- **Read more.** The full booklet has every derivation. Not needed for the lab.
