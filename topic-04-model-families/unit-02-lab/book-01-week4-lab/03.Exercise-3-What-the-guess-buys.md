## From the lecture

> **Red box 3:** *Stacking layers turns local looks into a view of the whole picture. Equivariance
> is not invariance; the ending decides which you get (0.998 vs 0.230). The guess fails when the
> data has no arrangement, when position matters, when things are far apart, or when the change
> is not a shift. On pre-centred data it buys nothing.*

## What you are doing

**In one sentence:** you will see how stacking stencils lets one number see the whole picture, how the ending of a network can keep or throw away the guess, and one case where the guess buys nothing.
