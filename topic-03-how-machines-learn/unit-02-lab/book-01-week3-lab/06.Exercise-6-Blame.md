## From the lecture

> **Red box 6:** *Backpropagation works out each weight's share of the blame for the loss, from
> the output backwards. It is the Blame step; gradient descent does the nudging. The PyTorch loop
> is five lines: zero_grad, forward, loss, backward, step.*

## What you are doing

Slide 39 walked blame backwards through the smallest network there is: one input, one hidden
neuron, one output, two dials. Here every number is printed: forward, check, blame, a check on the
blame, then one nudge. Then slide 42's five lines.
