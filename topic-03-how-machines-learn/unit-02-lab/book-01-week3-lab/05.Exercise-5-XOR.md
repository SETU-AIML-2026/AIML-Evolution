## From the lecture

> **Red box 5:** *A perceptron is one neuron and draws only a straight line. XOR is four dots no
> straight line separates. A hidden layer moves the dots so that a straight line does work. Each
> neuron needs a bend; without it, many layers collapse into one straight line.*

## What you are doing

Slide 33: the perceptron meets XOR and fails. Slide 34: two hidden neurons move the dots and it
works. Slide 35: the same two neurons with the bend switched off fail again. Three cells.
