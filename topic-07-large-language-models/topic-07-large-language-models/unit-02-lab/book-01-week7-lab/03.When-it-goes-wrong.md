# When It Goes Wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Red text under a cell | Read its last line. `SyntaxError`: a typo or missing bracket. `NameError`: a misspelt name, or you skipped a cell. `IndentationError`: line up the line with the ones around it. |
| "This notebook was not authored by Google" | Click **Run anyway**. It is the module's notebook. |
| Nothing happens when you press Shift + Enter | Click inside the grey box first, then press Shift and Enter together. |
| Your formula and solver lines do not agree | Check your law line and your formula for a typo or a wrong sign. |
| Strange results after changing something | Click **Runtime → Restart session** and run every cell again from the top. |
| `ollama` is "not recognized" or "command not found" | Close the window, open a new Command Prompt or Terminal, try again; if that fails, restart the laptop. |
