## Key Takeaways

1. **Learning is guess, check, blame, nudge, repeated.** Every model in this module is that loop with a different machine in the first box.
2. **A hidden layer moves the dots.** It turns a problem no straight line can solve (XOR, the moons) into one a straight line can. Without a bend between layers, depth buys nothing.
3. **The step size is the first thing to check.** Too small crawls; too big bounces, then explodes without an error message.
4. **Backprop is the chain rule, and nudging is its referee.** Never trust a backward pass you have not checked.
5. **A network is not magic.** On the credit data it barely beat a straight line, and accuracy still hid a low recall. Week 2's lessons about base rates and thresholds apply to every model, however deep.
