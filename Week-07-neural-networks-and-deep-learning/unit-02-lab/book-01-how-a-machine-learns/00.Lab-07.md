# Week 7 Lab: How a Machine Learns, and How It Is Wired

## Objectives

By the end of this lab, you'll be able to:

- Say what "learning" means in one sentence, and show it with two dials and three houses
- Compute one neuron by hand and in code, and explain why it can only ever draw one straight line
- Wire a tiny network by hand that solves XOR, and say what the hidden layer and the ReLU bend each do
- Build the forward pass of a 2 → 16 → 16 → 1 network in NumPy, and count its 337 dials
- Write the two losses, walk a dial downhill with gradient descent, and see what the step size does
- Find a dial's blame by nudging, write backpropagation, and prove it right with a gradient check
- Train your network on the two moons and on the Week 1 credit data, compare it with Week 2's logistic regression, and read its curves

Every section follows one part of the lecture, in the same order, with the same examples and the same numbers. The reference booklet explains each step on paper; this lab does it on your machine.

## Prerequisites

- The Week 7 lecture. Keep the reference booklet open beside you
- The Week 2 lab, where you built the logistic-regression default classifier. You will compare against it in Section 6
- Python 3.9+ with `numpy`, `pandas`, `scikit-learn` and `matplotlib`. Nothing new to install: the whole network is built from scratch in NumPy. PyTorch is only needed for the optional Exercise 7.5
- The **Week 07 Lab Files** archive on this unit: `UCI_Credit_Card.csv` (the same file as Weeks 1 to 5), `w7lab.py` (small helpers that load the data, so you can spend your time on the network) and `broken_run.py` (for Exercise 7.2)

> **Dataset and provenance.** Sections 2 to 6 use the **two moons**: 600 dots in two interlocking crescents, made by scikit-learn's `make_moons` with a fixed seed, 420 to learn from and 180 kept back. They are the dots from the lecture, so your numbers will match the slides. Section 6 then uses the **UCI "Default of Credit Card Clients"** dataset: Yeh, I. and Lien, C. (2009), *UCI Machine Learning Repository*, [https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H](https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H), licensed **CC BY 4.0**. `w7lab.load_credit()` splits it exactly as the Week 2 lab did (25% test, `random_state=0`, stratified) and scales it using the training part only.
