# When It Goes Wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your law-learner fails its success test | Fix it first with last week's four fixes, then race it |
| The example-learner's error does not fall as examples grow | A finding, not a mistake: noise in the examples limits it |
| Shrinking stops at the full size | Your law needs more numbers: say so in your finding |
| "Training got stuck... starting again" | Normal: the tools try a fresh start, up to three times |
| Red text under a cell | Read the last line; run the cells again from the top |
